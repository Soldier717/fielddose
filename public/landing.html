Clinical governance

FieldDose is built on the SWFL Regional Common Treatment Guidelines and operates as a training and reference tool under agency medical direction. Dosing changes ship only after physician sign-off, through a review process that produces a documented decision trail.

Patient worksheets never leave the medic's device: the ten most recent are kept, everything expires in seven days, and nothing is transmitted or stored server-side.
